# Translation Glossary — Simplified Chinese (zh-Hans)

Use concise, natural Simplified Chinese. Preserve good established wording. Translate complete meanings, including warning details. Keep placeholders, Markdown links, units, shortcuts, and identifiers intact. Use simplified characters throughout.

## Retained terms and formatting

Retain BetterDisplay, Apple, macOS, Metal, GPU, EDID, DisplayID, DDC, HDR, XDR, HiDPI, LoDPI, HDMI-CEC, SF Symbols, CUBE, LUT, Rec. 709, Rec. 2100 PQ, Display P3, DisplayLink Manager, and vendor/product identifiers as appropriate. Downspread is retained as a technical link capability term. Unit symbols and identifiers may intentionally match English; prose uses 字节 and 尼特 where natural.
