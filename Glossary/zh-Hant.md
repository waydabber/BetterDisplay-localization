## Retained terms and formatting

Retain product/standard identifiers such as DisplayLink Manager, Sidecar, Display P3, Rec. 709, Rec. 2100 PQ, sRGB, Downspread, Tag ID, GPU and protocol names. Preserve printf arguments, `${…}` interpolation names, Markdown links and their destinations. Manufacture dates use 年 and 週; week is not month.

- OSD, HDR, XDR, HiDPI, DDC, EDID, DisplayID, Metal, LUT: Technical names
