## Retained terms

- framebuffer, compositor, Metal, LUT, HDR, SDR, XDR, HiDPI, LoDPI, DDC, EDID, DisplayID, UUID, CLI, API, HTTP, HDMI-CEC, PIP: Technical identifiers and standard abbreviations.
- Picture in Picture, Screen (blend mode), Downspread, Sepia, Vignette, X-Ray, Chrome, Fade, Instant, Noir, Process, Transfer: Established feature, effect, blend-mode, or technical names. “Screen” here is a blend operation, not a display.
