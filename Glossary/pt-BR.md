# Translation Glossary — Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR)

Prefer natural, concise Brazilian Portuguese; retain good existing wording, including capitalization unless misleading.

## Conventions

- Retain technical identifiers: framebuffer, Metal, HDR, SDR, XDR, HiDPI, LoDPI, EDID, DDC, UUID and OSD.
- Intentionally retained source-equal values include product and protocol names (Apple, Apple Silicon, Intel, Philips Smart TV, Yamaha AVR, Display P3, Display Stream Compression (DSC), CGDirectDisplayID, LG alt), technical labels (Downspread, Underscan, Rec. 709, Rec. 2100 PQ and acronyms), units/numbers/formats/URLs/placeholders, and identical Portuguese words such as Experimental, Horizontal, Vertical, Menu, Original, Virtual and Volume.
- Wake-on-LAN, WOL, WoWLAN and broadcast retain their technical names.
