# Translation Glossary — German (de)

## Conventions

Use informal **du**, lowercase except at the beginning of a sentence, consistently throughout the interface.

Use natural German UI wording. Keep actions concise and use „möchten“ for confirmation questions. Preserve accurate existing terminology; do not reproduce English syntax when it sounds unnatural.

- Keep **Display** for display, **Bildschirm** for screen and **Monitor** for monitor where natural.
- **EDID** is feminine when referring to data/configuration: benutzerdefinierte EDID. **LUT** is feminine (die Lookup-Tabelle); **LUT-Filter** is masculine because Filter is the head noun.
- Spell **softwarebasiert** as one word, lowercase inside sentences and parentheses.
- Rotation angles use **°**, never **º**.

## Retained terms

Keep Tri-Screen as the product configuration name. Standby, Dark Wake, LUT, Metal, Compositor, DDC, HDR, XDR, SDR, HiDPI and LoDPI remain technical identifiers. Explain Dark Wake as waking with the screen off in detailed help. CUBE and LUT_3D_SIZE remain exact file-format identifiers.

Intentionally unchanged terms include Bloom, Framerate, Overlay, Downspread, Display Stream Compression, technical acronyms, units, product names, protocol values and the compact LG alt heading.
